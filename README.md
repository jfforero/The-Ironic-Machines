## 📖 Project Overview
**The Ironic Machine[s]** is a practice-based research project exploring the intersection of **Speech Emotion Recognition (SER)** and the procedural generation of **Affective Virtual Environments (AVE)**. The core of the research investigates "Prosodic Irony"—the mismatch between *what* is said (semantics) and *how* it is said (acoustics)—translating these complex emotional nuances into digital spatiality.

---

## 📂 Repository Structure

The project is divided into two primary "situations" documenting the scientific methodology and the creative implementation:

### ⚙️ Technical Situation
This section serves as the analytical backbone and contains the **Google Colab notebooks** utilized for the research phase.
* **Chap 4: Section 1:** Semantic Sentiment Analysis (Rocchio, LSTM, and BERT-based classifiers).
* **Chap 4: Section 2:** Acoustic Speech Emotion Recognition (MLP, CNN, and CNN+LSTM models using eGeMAPS/MFCC).
* **Chap 5: Section 1:** Assessing Irony (Statistical inference and perceptual evaluation frameworks).
* **Chap 6: Section 1:** Affective Music Generation (Mapping emotional valence/arousal to musical features).
* **Chap 7: Section 2:** Affective Virtual Environment Generation (Three-level prompt engineering framework).
* **EXTRA:** Lightweight LSTM trained with 40 Mel-Frequency Cepstral Coefficients (MFCC) for real-time Hugging Face/web deployment.

### 🎭 Practical Situation
This section documents the transition from speech input to immersive virtual experiences through **nine distinct iterations**:

1. **Iteration 1: What Stirs Within** – Initial exploration of voice-to-space mapping.
2. **Iteration 2: A Máquina Sensível** – Real-time voice interaction with transforming geometry.
3. **Iteration 3: Abandoned Memories** – Exploring "home" and nostalgia through speech-driven decay.
4. **Iteration 4: Veintitantos Vientos** – 24 equirectangular panoramas based on recited poetry.
5. **Iteration 5: En train d’oublier** – A web-art project exploring memory in the Salar de Uyuni.
6. **Iteration 6: Terra Australis Ignota** – Speculative emotional cartography (Exhibited at Florence XR 2024).
7. **Iteration 7: The What Space** – A VR environment analyzing the emotional variability of the word "What".
8. **Iteration 8: The Ironic Machine[s]** – Primary implementation of the cross-modal irony framework.
9. **Iteration 9: Bello** – Final system mapping poetry into 360° evolving affective landscapes.
